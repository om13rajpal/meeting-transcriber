Built from your four references — PixelAI's collage confidence, Peppermint's warm illustrated fintech, Tamagotchi's pixel nostalgia, Sayings' quiet editorial split-screen — reworked around what this product actually is, not a generic "AI SaaS" template.

Product read

What it is: upload a recording → ffmpeg + Deepgram Nova‑3 diarize a Hinglish conversation → a private, searchable transcript with speaker names, tags, and cost-per-minute you can trust.

Who's using it: a solo founder, consultant, or student who records their own meetings — not an enterprise buyer, not a social feed. One person, private history, real recordings.

What the interface must earn: trust (this is someone's private conversation), calm during the wait (ffmpeg + Deepgram takes real minutes), and a genuine payoff the moment "Transcribing…" flips to done.

Where it lives today: no landing page exists — first-time visitors land straight on /login. All five directions below design that missing front door.
