§ Changelog · wireframe to production
  • The grey box became evidence. The structure pass had one rectangle labelled "illustration". Login now carries a real paper transcript card with the DIARISED stamp, a highlighter mark and one redacted phone number. Signup carries the locked hand off ribbon, inlined from assets/handoff-ribbon.svg rather than redrawn.
  • Labels moved to the intake sheet. Field labels are IBM Plex Mono at 11px with 0.14em tracking, so the form reads like a document you fill in, not a settings panel.
  • Every placeholder became a real value. priya.bansal@northbeam.in, a meeting that ran 41 minutes 12 seconds, $0.2925 of Nova-3, three speakers with names.
  • The error state stopped guessing. It says "Invalid email or password" and nothing more, on purpose. Naming which half was wrong would tell an attacker which addresses have accounts here.
  • Submit grew a real in flight state. The label swaps to "Checking credentials", a 2px highlighter sweep runs along the bottom edge, and the whole fieldset locks so a second Enter cannot fire a second request. No spinner anywhere.
  • Focus got its 2px back. Every other line on these screens is a 1px hairline. The focus ring is the single exception, stamp red at 2px with an animated offset, which is exactly what a focus indicator should be.

§ 03 · Buttons

Primary · rest
Primary · hover

Fill moves --cr-red-fill to --cr-red-fill-hover. The glow stays put.

Primary · pressed

scale(0.97) over --cr-dur-press 140ms, glow drops to nothing. Every pressable element in this system does this, with no exceptions.

Primary · in flight

No spinner. A 2px highlighter sweep runs the bottom edge on a 1100ms loop and the label states what is happening. Under reduced motion the sweep freezes at 60 percent and stops looping.

Google · rest

Outline, never filled. In this brand the primary action is already red, so a second filled button would compete with it.

Google · leaving for consent

This is a full page navigation to /api/auth/google, not a popup. The button holds the label until the browser leaves.

§ 04 · Messages

Login failure · the only message this form is allowed to send
!

Invalid email or passwordCheck both and try again. If you cannot remember it, reset the password.

Never "no account with that email", never "wrong password". The message is identical whether the address exists or not, because a different message for each is a free account enumeration tool. This is a security rule from the project's own CLAUDE.md, not a copy preference, so it is not open to a friendlier rewrite.

Google unavailable
!

Google sign in is unavailableUse your email and password for now.

Only reachable by hitting /api/auth/google directly with a stale bookmark. In the normal case the button does not render at all.

§ 05 · Edge cases and variants

Google not configured § Sign in
Email
PasswordForgot password?

When GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID is unset, OAuthButtons returns null. The button and the Or rule both disappear and the form simply starts at Email. There is no greyed out button and no "coming soon".

Session check · first paint

Skeletons match the real field geometry, 38px tall inputs and a 48px button, not generic grey blocks. Opacity pulses 0.4 to 0.8 over 1200ms. Mostly theoretical here, since the page is server rendered and usually paints complete.

Keyboard map

Tab walks Google, then email, then forgot, then password, then reveal, then submit.
Enter in any field submits.
Esc dismisses the error banner, with zero animation.

Escape closes instantly on purpose. Keyboard initiated actions are never animated in this product, because they repeat all day and motion makes them feel broken.

Reduced motion

The headline stops staggering and simply appears. The ribbon stops drawing itself. The submit sweep freezes. Everything that carries meaning, colour, focus, and the error banner's own appearance, is kept.

Fewer and gentler, not none.

Narrow · under 1040px

The split collapses to one column. The evidence panel stays on top but drops to its own band with a hairline under it, and the form runs full width beneath. The transcript card keeps its shadow, since it is the one sheet of paper in the system.
